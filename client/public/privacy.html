Resurviv Privacy Policy

This policy describes account and gameplay data handled by Resurviv at resurviv.biz.

Want to delete your account? Visit our Resurviv Account Deletion page for instructions, details of what is deleted and retained, and Discord support. You can delete your account from the browser game without installing the app.

Personal Identification Information

We may collect personal identification information from Users in various ways, including when they voluntarily provide it or through our authentication systems. We collect the following types of information:

  • Username chosen by players when they interact with our game
  • Account identifiers, profile names, linked Google or Discord sign-in identifiers and sessions
  • IP addresses and connection details in security logs, and hashed IP addresses for ban enforcement and certain reward checks

We use this information for gameplay, account management, rewards and security enforcement. Sign-in providers may return an email address and other profile information during authentication. Resurviv checks whether the provider reports a verified email, but does not store email addresses in its account records. If you contact the support team, the information you provide is also available to that team.

Game-Related Data Collection

In addition to personal identification information, we collect and store the following game-related data associated with user accounts:

  • Virtual items owned by the user (including skins, emotes, and other customization options)
  • Complete match history (records of all games played with the account)
  • Friends, clan memberships and messages, GP balances, gifts, market transactions, auctions, rewards, and pass and quest progress
  • Competitive results, ratings, tournament predictions and bets, and stored arena recordings when replay storage is enabled

This data is stored securely and is used to enhance the gaming experience, track player progression, and enable account-specific features.

Non-Personal Identification Information

We may collect non-personal identification information whenever Users interact with our Site. This may include the browser name, device type, operating system, Internet service provider details, and other technical data related to how Users interact with our Site.

Web Browser Cookies

Resurviv uses cookies and browser storage for sign-in, account state and game preferences. Advertising services may also use cookies as described below. You can manage cookies and site data through your browser; blocking them can affect sign-in and other features. Deleting your Resurviv account does not itself clear every preference or third-party cookie stored on your device.

How We Use Collected Information

Resurviv uses information for the following purposes:

  • To improve the functionality and user experience of our Site
  • To respond to requests made through the Discord support server
  • To support gameplay features such as usernames and account management
  • To authenticate users through Discord and Google login systems
  • To manage user accounts and maintain game progression data
  • To enforce security measures, including IP bans when necessary
  • To maintain game integrity and prevent abuse

How We Protect Your Information

We adopt suitable data collection, storage, and processing practices as well as security measures to safeguard your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Specifically:

  • IP ban records use hashed IP addresses; security logs also contain raw IP addresses
  • Daily cleanup is scheduled to remove security logs older than 30 days
  • Linked sign-in identities support authentication and account linking; Discord information can also be used to check eligibility for Discord-related rewards

Account Deletion and Data Retention

To delete your Resurviv account, sign in, open Account Settings, select Delete Account, enter DELETE, and select Confirm. This concerns your Resurviv account, not your Google or Discord account. For assistance, contact the team through the Resurviv Discord support server.

Successful deletion removes your account and its linked sign-in, session, friend, inventory, progress, reward and other account-linked records. A clan you own is also deleted. Match history is disconnected from your account but retains player names and gameplay results; it is not fully anonymized. Separately stored competitive and tournament history, published records and some administrative or transaction records can also retain names or identifiers without an automatic expiry.

Security logs remain until the daily cleanup removes entries older than 30 days. Temporary IP bans stop applying at their expiry and are deleted when checked after expiry; permanent IP bans have no automatic expiry. Shared arena replay files, when enabled, remain until their scheduled expiry and subsequent daily cleanup (30 days from upload by default, subject to the server's replay retention setting). Account deletion does not erase recordings or transaction logs already sent to Discord.

The Account Deletion page provides the detailed breakdown of removed and retained records. Contact the support team to request help with retained information.

Reset Stats keeps your account and pass progress while removing the link to your match history. It does not delete the underlying match records or stored player names and is not a complete erasure of gameplay data.

Sharing Your Personal Information

Profiles, leaderboards, match results and other public game features display player names and gameplay information to other users. Friends and clan features share information with the relevant players. Service providers handle data needed for hosting, authentication, advertising and support.

When Discord transaction logging is enabled, market purchases and gifts send player profile identifiers, item or GP details and transaction times to configured Discord channels. These messages are not removed by account deletion and have no automatic expiry in Resurviv. Information you submit to Discord support is also subject to Discord's privacy practices.

Third-Party Authentication

We use Discord and Google OAuth2 for sign-in. These services provide a user identifier and authentication information, including verified-email status. Discord may also supply profile and server-tag information for reward checks. We store linked provider identifiers, not your Google or Discord password, and do not store provider email addresses in account records. These services have their own privacy policies.

Advertising

Ads served on our Site may be delivered by third-party advertising partners who may use cookies to track information about browsers and visits. These cookies help deliver advertisements believed to be relevant to Users.

We use third-party advertising companies to serve ads. These companies may use information about your visits to this and other websites to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you. For more information, visit the Network Advertising Initiative.

For Google's advertising data practices and controls, see Google's advertising information.

Children's Privacy

Our Site and services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete such information from our servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Resurviv reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. When updated, the revised date will be displayed at the bottom of this page. Users are encouraged to check this page frequently to stay informed about any changes. Your continued use of the Site following updates to this policy constitutes your acknowledgment and acceptance of those changes.

Contacting Us

For privacy questions, account deletion or requests concerning retained data, contact the team through the Resurviv Discord support server. Do not post passwords, sign-in codes or sensitive account information in public channels.
