Resurviv Account Deletion

You can delete your Resurviv account from the game in your web browser or in the app. You do not need to install the app or join Discord to use the browser option.

Open Resurviv to delete your account Get deletion help on Discord

How to delete your account

  1. Sign in to the Resurviv account you want to delete.
  2. Open Account Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Enter DELETE and select Confirm.

After a successful deletion, you are signed out. This deletes your Resurviv account; it does not delete your Google or Discord account.

What is deleted

Successful account deletion removes your profile, linked sign-in identities, sessions, friends and friend requests, inventory and loadout, GP balance, pass and quest progress, and account-linked gift, reward, market listing, auction bid, prediction and betting records. Your clan membership, join requests, clan messages and account-linked clan stats are removed. If you own a clan, the clan and its linked records are also deleted. Your link to stored arena replays is removed.

What remains and for how long

  • Match history: the link to your account is removed, but match results, player names and gameplay statistics remain. These records have no automatic expiry; they are not fully anonymized.
  • Competitive and tournament history: separately stored results, rosters, ratings and historical leaderboards may retain names or account identifiers. They have no automatic expiry.
  • Security logs: IP addresses, player names, account identifiers and connection details remain until the daily cleanup removes logs older than 30 days.
  • Ban records: hashed IP addresses and moderation details are retained separately. Temporary IP bans stop applying at their expiry and are removed when checked after expiry. Permanent bans have no automatic expiry.
  • Stored arena replays, when enabled: shared recordings may still contain your player name and gameplay. They remain until their scheduled expiry and subsequent daily cleanup. The default retention is 30 days from upload; the server's replay retention setting can differ.
  • Other shared records: another player's market transaction can remain after its link to your account is cleared. Published news, moderation and administrative records, and transaction logs sent to Discord may retain names or identifiers. Account deletion does not remove these records; no automatic expiry is set for them.

Reset Stats without deleting your account

In Account Settings, select Reset Stats, enter RESET STATS, and select Confirm. This removes the link between your account and its match history while keeping your account and pass progress. It does not delete the match records, stored player names, competitive records or security logs. It is not a complete deletion of gameplay data.

Need help or cannot sign in?

Join the Resurviv Discord support server and ask the team for help with account deletion or removal of retained data. Tell the team which Resurviv account your request concerns; ownership verification may be needed. Do not post passwords, sign-in codes or sensitive account information in public channels. Discord assistance requires a Discord account.
